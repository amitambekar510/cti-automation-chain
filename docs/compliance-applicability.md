# Regulatory and framework applicability — 12 references

These are potential **control and evidence mappings**, not twelve certifications or universal CTI mandates. Verify current clauses, commencement and sector scope with the appropriate owner.

| Reference | CTI / detection evidence angle | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| CERT-In Directions 2022 | Incident records, time synchronization, reporting and log retention | Requirements depend on entity and incident |
| SEBI CSCRF 2024 | Threat intelligence, monitoring, detection and response | SEBI-regulated entity categories vary |
| RBI IT and Cyber | Risk-based cyber monitoring and incident management | Regulated entity-specific |
| DPDP Act 2023 | Personal data minimization and breach handling | Verify commenced provisions |
| NSE/BSE technology audit | Monitoring and change-control evidence | Exchange/member-specific |
| NPCI/UPI | Payment ecosystem cyber controls and evidence | Participant-specific |
| SEBI PFUTP 2003 | Securities fraud context for investigations | Not a general CTI automation mandate |
| ISO/IEC 27001:2022 | A.5.7 threat intelligence, A.8.15 logging, A.8.16 monitoring | Subject to ISMS scope and SoA |
| ISO/IEC 27090 | Identifier/title and relevance not verified | No conformity claim |
| MITRE ATT&CK | TTP mapping and detection coverage hypotheses | Knowledge base, not a regulation |
| ISO/IEC 42001:2023 | AI governance for model-assisted CTI workflows | Only if AI management system applies |
| NIST CSF 2.0 | Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover | Framework alignment, not certification |

Evidence examples: data source provenance, collection approvals, analyst review, prompt/model version, hallucination checks, detection tests, false positives, change tickets, SITREPs and management signoff.

Official starting points: https://www.cert-in.org.in/ , https://www.sebi.gov.in/ , https://www.rbi.org.in/ , https://www.meity.gov.in/ , https://www.iso.org/ , https://attack.mitre.org/ , https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework .
